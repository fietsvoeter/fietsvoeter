## Gravel Schoenen Versus Racefiets En MTB Schoenen

Begrijpen hoe gravel schoenen verschillen van race en MTB schoenen helpt juiste keuze maken. Gravel discipline borrowt eigenschappen van beide maar is unique category. Neither pure road nor pure MTB schoenen zijn optimal voor gravel.

Cleat systeem is primaire onderscheid. Racefiets schoenen gebruiken driegaats cleats zoals **Shimano** SPD-SL of **Look** Keo. Deze cleats steken uit zool making walking awkward. Grote platform maximizes power transfer maar sacrifices walkability. Gravel en MTB schoenen gebruiken tweegaats SPD cleats recessed in zool. Walking is natural, pedaling efficiency is slightly lower.

Zool stijfheid varies across categories. Racefiets schoenen have carbon soles maximizing stiffness typically rating 10 tot 12 on stiffness index. Gravel schoenen range 6 tot 9 providing good power transfer while maintaining some flex for comfort. Full MTB schoenen rate 5 tot 7 prioritizing walking comfort. For gravel, middle ground is optimal balancing efficiency with practicality.

Tread pattern determines off-bike capability. Racefiets schoenen have minimal tread, just small rubber pads. Useless for walking on anything but smooth surfaces. MTB schoenen have aggressive lugs for mud traction. Gravel schoenen have moderate tread adequate for gravel paths and café floors without being overkill. Voor Nederlandse hardpacked gravel, aggressive MTB tread is unnecessary.

Closure systems affect adjustability and durability. Traditional laces are simple and cheap maar loosen during rides. Velcro straps are adjustable maar wear out. Ratchet buckles are durable maar limited adjustment. **BOA** dial systems provide micro-adjustment and are field serviceable. Most premium gravel schoenen use BOA for convenience. Budget options use velcro or laces.

Weight consideration differs by discipline. Racefiets riders obsess over 50 gram weight difference. Gravel riders accept heavier shoes for durability and functionality. Typical gravel shoe weighs 300 tot 350 gram per shoe. Ultra-light XC MTB schoenen get to 250 gram maar sacrifice protection. Extra weight is imperceptible tijdens actual gravel riding given variable terrain.

Carbon-reinforced sole provides 8 out of 10 stiffness adequate voor hard efforts without excessive rigidity. **BOA** closure system allows precise fit adjustment. Stiffness from carbon-reinforced nylon sole transmits power efficiently. Not full carbon stiffness van S-Phyre road schoenen maar substantial improvement over budget gravel schoenen. Michelin outsole rubber compound provides outstanding grip on wet surfaces. Tread pattern balances pedaling efficiency with walking traction. ## SPD Cleat Setup En Afstelling

Correct cleat positioning prevents injuries and maximizes comfort. Gravel riding involves long hours in saddle making proper fit critical. Understanding cleat adjustment principles helps achieve optimal setup. Small adjustments make big difference in knee alignment and pedaling efficiency.

Fore-aft position determines power application point. Ball of foot should be over pedal spindle. Too far forward causes calf strain. Too far back reduces power transfer. Start with cleat center 10mm behind ball of foot. Test ride adjusting forward or back based on feel. Most riders end up within 5mm range of this starting point.

Float allows foot to rotate naturally during pedal stroke. SPD cleats typically offer 4 tot 6 degrees float depending on cleat model. Multi-release cleats have more float than single-release. For new riders or those with knee issues, maximum float reduces injury risk. Experienced riders might prefer less float for more direct feel. Start with more float reducing gradually if desired.

Angle adjustment aligns cleat with natural foot angle. Stand naturally, observe toe-out angle. Set cleat matching this angle. If knees hurt after rides, angle is probably wrong. Small adjustments of 2 tot 3 degrees make significant difference. Don't force feet into unnatural angles causing knee stress. Proper alignment feels natural not forced.

Cleat wear requires regular inspection. Worn cleats cause premature release endangering rider. Metal contact points on cleats wear from repeated engagement. Replace cleats when wear visible or clipping becomes unreliable. Typical cleat life is 2000 tot 4000 kilometer depending on walking amount. Regular inspection prevents unexpected failures.

Tension adjustment on pedals determines release force. New riders benefit from loose tension allowing easy exit. Experienced riders prefer firmer tension preventing accidental release. Most SPD pedals have allen key adjustable tension. Test tension in safe environment before committing to trail riding. Ability to unclip quickly is safety issue.
